Two ways to make Life fun

Replacements for the squiggly trail line. Each idea comes in two takes. Everything is hand-drawn SVG and CSS 3D, no stock images. The words are draft copy for you to rewrite.

1AThe coffee ceremony, poured by scrolling

Scroll and the jebena pours the three traditional rounds. Each cup is a chapter of your story.

Keep scrolling to pour

1BThe coffee ceremony, you pour

Visitors tap a cup (or the button) and pour each round themselves. Shorter on the page and more playful.

2APostcards from home, the fanned deck

Four postcards fanned like a hand of cards. Move your mouse over one to tilt it in 3D, and click to flip it over and read the back.

Tip: the hills inside each card shift at different depths as it tilts.

2BPostcards from home, on the line

The same cards pinned to a clothesline, swaying a little. Hover to steady one, and click to flip it.
