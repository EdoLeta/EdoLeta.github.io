Sesame Street, in Amharic

Translating Sesame Street friends into Amharic so Ethiopian children meet them in their own language.

Role
Translator
Languages
English to Amharic
Audience
Ethiopian children, broadcast

The challenge

Children’s dialogue is deceptively hard to translate. Jokes, rhyme and rhythm carry the teaching, so a literal translation loses the lesson along with the laugh.
